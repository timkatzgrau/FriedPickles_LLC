About Fried Pickles LLC

Small company.
Practical purpose.

The best ideas often start with a simple question: why is this so hard to find?

Founded by Tim Katzgrau, Fried Pickles LLC is an independent software company building tools for everyday discovery. ChillMaps began with the search for better non-alcoholic options. RepairSeeker brings that same focus to finding local repair services.

We build and operate our own products, with a focus on useful information, straightforward experiences, and a direct connection to the people who use them.
